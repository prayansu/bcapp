About Bro Code

A minimal online clipboard. Paste text, hit Send, and share the 3-character code with someone else. It expires 5 minutes after saving, and is deleted the moment it's retrieved.

Your "Recent notes" history is saved only in this browser, on this device, so you can find things you've sent before — it isn't sent anywhere.
